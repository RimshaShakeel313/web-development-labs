WELCOME TO PAKISTAN

The Land of Pure People

Pakistan is a beautiful country located in South Asia. It became independent on 14 August 1947. Islamabad is the capital city of Pakistan. Pakistan is famous for its beautiful mountains, rivers, historical places and rich culture.

Some Beautiful Places of Pakistan

Pakistan has many beautiful tourist destinations, including Hunza, Skardu, Swat, Murree and Neelum Valley.

National Anthem of Pakistan

The National Anthem of Pakistan is a symbol of national unity, pride and patriotism. It reminds us of our love for our homeland.
